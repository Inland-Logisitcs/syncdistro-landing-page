## 2. Description of the Service

SyncDistro is a tool specialized in field sales management. It allows you to:

- Record and manage customers and prospects, with their geographic location and their documents.
- Plan visit routes, optimize the order of stops and assign them to a rep with an execution date.
- Run routes from the mobile app, log visits with their outcome and, optionally, record voice notes
  that are transcribed and analyzed automatically.
- Browse the product catalog obtained from the Organization's ERP.
- Build orders from visits and send them to the Organization's ERP.
- Supervise commercial activity from the web system, including the progress of routes under way and
  the visit history.

## 3. What the Service does not include

does not handle invoicing, inventory management, delivery logistics or order collection. Those
processes are the responsibility of the external systems the Organization already uses.

SyncDistro acts as a specialized interface for commercial operations and delivers orders to the ERP
for subsequent processing.
